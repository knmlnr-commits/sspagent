Incident Priority Levels

PriorityLevelDescription
P1Urgent / CriticalComplete work stoppage affecting a critical function or broad group of users. No workaround available.
P2High / MajorBusiness functions severely degraded, multiple users impacted, or key customer affected. Workaround may exist but is not easily sustainable.
P3Normal / ModerateCertain functions unavailable or system degraded. Workaround may be available.
P4Low / MinimalLittle impact on normal business. Workaround is available. Handled on a scheduled basis.

Priority is determined by Impact x Urgency

ImpactDescription
PersonA single user is affected
TeamA minimal number of staff affected; acceptable service requires extra effort
DepartmentA moderate number of staff unable to do their job properly
BuildingBusiness functions severely degraded at building level
CompanyA large number of staff affected and/or unable to work

P1 and some P2 incidents are major incidents. After resolution:

Outage < 60 minutes: Root cause shared via email or Teams.

Outage > 60 minutes: Full RCA document provided within 10 working days.
